## Voice card

Voice: technical-dry (copy-registers.md 2)
Pairs: exact, not cold; sure, not loud; no joke in the hero, steps or pricing
Person: "you" for what the buyer gets, "we" for the work; the product is the subject in feature lines
Sentences: mostly 8 to 16 words; one longer sentence where it explains
Humour: none
House words: screen, flow, component, Figma — the same word every time
Never: virtue words about ourselves, a pun on the name, a strip of invented figures
